Prayer Times Desktop v1.3.3
- Available right now is now the primary section: one large full-width card per event (small label, large event name, large countdown); the Next event card keeps its previous size below it.
- Forbidden time: an interval (sunrise, Zawal, before sunset) is shown only while it is in progress, in a violet card (heading 'Forbidden time' / 'নিষিদ্ধ সময়', 'Praying is not allowed until 11:48 AM', range, 'Ends in' countdown). No advance notice, and an interval is never a Next event target: Next event is always the soonest obligatory or voluntary prayer.
- Daily prayers cards: a quiet 'in 3h 54m' / 'ends in 25m' line (Bangla: '৩ ঘণ্টা ৫৪ মিনিট পরে' / '২৫ মিনিটে শেষ'), hours and minutes only, refreshed every minute, nothing once the prayer has passed.
- Sky panel redesigned: one SVG scene whose sun/moon travel linearly in time from sunrise to sunset (height = sine), with violet bands for the prohibited intervals drawn at their exact proportional width; four time-of-day palettes, layered stars and clouds, theme tokens only.
- Prayer descriptions stays one section; its categories are now rows stacked vertically and sized to their content (evidence-first cards kept).
- Settings > Home page: show/hide Prayer descriptions, Sunnah & Seerah and On this day (default shown, saved, English/Bangla). A hidden section is not rendered and runs no timers.
- The Local timetable section is gone from the home page. The calculation method, Asr method, time zone and the prohibited-interval times moved into Settings (Calculation profile). Dead code, CSS and translation keys removed; tests updated.
- Today's progress line: every time the window is shown again from the tray (Rust-emitted event, visibilitychange or focus after being hidden) a NEW line is chosen from the grammar; never the one just shown, recent lines are remembered, always accurate to the progress state. It does not change while the window stays visible, except on events.
- Windows 10 notifications: root cause found before any change (see TEST-RESULTS.md). The plugin tags every toast with the bundle identifier, which Windows only honours when a Start Menu shortcut with that AppUserModelID exists, and it throws away the send result, so a missing shortcut means a silent drop. Toasts now go through our own native command (notify_native) that uses the app identity when the shortcut exists and the PowerShell identity otherwise, and returns the real error. A failed reminder is retried instead of being marked sent. New 'Send test notification' button in Settings shows the result and a diagnosis.

Prayer Times Desktop v1.3.2
- Today's progress line: a warm, human line generated offline (plan -> sentence plan -> per-language realization; English and natural colloquial Bangla with Bengali digits). It follows a fixed cycle: a warm reaction right after a tick (about 15 minutes), then plain progress facts only ("Fajr done, 2 + 2, full set"), a window open, and a heads-up for the next prayer only inside a window learned from your habit (default about 20-45 minutes before it, shorter if you usually tick late; never for batch-tickers), all done, Friday Jumu'ah. There is no wording about missed prayers at all. Ramadan/Eid/date-specific headlines keep priority. About 398,000 English and 426,000 Bangla distinct lines (sentence patterns with synonym alternatives and clause recombinations; counted by script), chosen deterministically by date with a capped memory so lines do not repeat for weeks; the line changes only on events. No citations, no mention of God or the Prophet, no counts of what was missed, no percentages, no "1 of 5"; nothing is said on days with nothing ticked.
- Offline habit learning (last 14 days): habit strength per prayer, usual time, batch-ticker detection, a weekly trend shown only when it is up, and positive milestones. Heads-ups fade for prayers that are almost never ticked and for batch-tickers. Rare Sunnah/Nafl suggestions (Duha, Awwabin; never Fard or Tahajjud; only if skipped yesterday; never two days running; with a short heads-up only in the 10 minutes before they open, and again while open). Resetting history resets what is learned. Ticks now also store when they were made (old history loads unchanged).
- The 365 daily reflections moved from the progress card to the Sunnah & Seerah carousel ("Daily reflection" / "আজকের ভাবনা").
- Daily prayer cards: the sub-tick row is replaced by a short bilingual note with its source. Sunni schools (Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) share one evidence set (Qur'an, Bukhari, Muslim and the Sunan); Ja'fari has its own (Qur'an and al-Kafi). No reference is invented. The reference section has its own reading-oriented cards (name, the Qur'an or hadith as a quote with its reference line, guidance and rak'ah quieter). Ja'fari hides Duha, Awwabin and Tarawih and Hanafi-specific guidance.
- "Next prayer" is now "Next event" (পরবর্তী ইভেন্ট): the hero counts down to the next event of any kind from one ordered timeline (obligatory and voluntary prayers, prohibited intervals, the Ja'fari midnight), with "Next: Prohibited time" / "পরবর্তী: নিষিদ্ধ সময়" in the calm Zawal style (Ja'fari: disliked/মাকরুহ wording).
- Fixed: the hero could sit at 00:00:00 with a stale "Current: Isha" after a prayer began. Root cause: it was rebuilt only by render(). Now a one-shot timer fires at the exact next boundary, the 1 s tick checks, and clock jumps (sleep/wake, hidden-window throttling, clock set back, a new day) are detected. Regression tests with faked dates cover the exact second, Isha to Fajr, Ja'fari midnight and every prayer.
- Layout: "Available right now . Prayer events" is always the first block of the page (calm empty state included), with the next-event card directly under it. Nothing reorders on a tick.
- Prohibited intervals between prayer events: an active interval, or one that begins before the next prayer event, is shown as its own compact row between the available event and a small "Next prayer event" row, with start and end and a live countdown while active (gold style, bilingual, Ja'fari makruh wording).
- Locked ticks: a marked event (with its Fard, Sunnah and Nafl parts) can be unmarked only until the next prayer event begins; Isha, Witr, Qiyam and Tahajjud lock at the next Fajr; Eid and Tarawih lock when the next event starts. Ticking rules are unchanged. Locked items keep their colours and show a small padlock with a tooltip ("Locked after Dhuhr began" / "যোহর শুরু হওয়ার পর লক হয়েছে"); click and keyboard do nothing, with no error sound or message. Locks are remembered, so a clock set back or a time-zone change never re-opens them. The weekly view marks locked days. Older history loads unchanged.
- Standalone voluntary prayers (Duha, Awwabin, Qiyam, Tahajjud, Witr, Tarawih, Eid) have a single tick only; sub-ticks exist only for the five Fard prayers. History with sub-part data for a voluntary prayer is folded into the single tick when read.
- Sky panel under the header (the top clock): a wide, short panel framed by thin lines. The sun crosses a dashed arc from sunrise to sunset (a glowing disc with rays, half-set at the horizon near the edges, brightest at midday); the moon crosses it from moonrise to moonset in its current phase (faint and translucent by day); the sky colour follows the sun's height (deep indigo at night, warm dawn and dusk, a soft day sky, all mixed from the theme tokens, light and dark); 44 deterministic stars twinkle at night and fade out by civil twilight; three soft clouds drift by day, warm at dawn/dusk, faint at night. All offline (sun and moon position, rise and set, illumination and waxing/waning from latitude, longitude and the clock), redrawn about once a minute; twinkle and drift are CSS transform/opacity only, paused while the window is hidden and static with reduced motion.
- Fixed: the Karachi/Hanafi label under the timetable ignored the chosen profile; Bangla translation of the word "events" corrupted English text; untranslated "SUNNAH & SEERAH" heading and source names in Bangla; the reference history/source text of the old guidance is no longer shown (replaced by evidence).
- Fixed (found while testing the lock): an undefined variable in the Sunnah/Nafl untick path.
